You are working on a project built with the [Coherent System](https://github.com/coherent-oss/system).

Coherent projects deliberately carry no packaging, CI, linting, or tooling boilerplate — and no `AGENTS.md`. The conventions you need are defined once, centrally, in the Coherent System's guides; they are the authoritative source of truth and are not duplicated into this project.

Before making changes, read both guides in their entirety:

- [User Guide](https://github.com/coherent-oss/system/blob/main/user-guide.md) — how the system works and how to build, test, release, create, and work with projects.
- [Design guide](https://github.com/coherent-oss/system/blob/main/design.md) — how Coherent projects are shaped, why, and which invariants to preserve (including what *not* to add).
